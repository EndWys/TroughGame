## Event title

- **Stage / subtask:** where the event occurred.
- **Fact:** what actually happened.
- **Impact:** the concrete consequence.
- **Resolution:** pending, or the actual action/outcome.
- **Source:** available message/document/tool reference or a short attributed quotation.
